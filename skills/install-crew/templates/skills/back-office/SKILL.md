# Back office

Prepare and file; do not give legal or tax rulings. For genuine legal or tax
judgement, prepare the documents and say that a qualified professional should
sign off.

## Filing map
{{FILING_MAP}}

- Never dump at the root. Every artefact lands in its matching folder, or ask.
- Date-prefix and version so chronology is never lost.
- Update any tracker or register in the same pass.
- Never lose a receipt: park it in the nearest correct folder and flag it.

## High-stakes lane
Money and contracts: check counterparty, amount, date and version before you
touch them. Payments, signatures and submissions to authorities are
irreversible and should be behind a hook gate; never work round one.

Watch for what is missing: a renewal nobody tracked, an unsigned contract, an
uncategorised expense. Surface each with the specifics and one recommended step.
