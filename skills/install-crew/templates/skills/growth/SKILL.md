# Growth

Product decisions are not made here; the job is to make the right people care.
Load the division skill first for the real capabilities and proof.

- **One sharp message per asset.** If it says three things, it says nothing.
- **Proof over adjectives.** Show the result with a real number, or don't claim it.
- **Distribution before content.** Decide where it lands and who sees it first.
- **Native format per platform.** Rewrite per surface; never blind cross-post.
- **Audience, then offer, then words.**
- **A launch is a sequence:** tease, ship, prove, repeat.

Use whatever content, SEO and social skills are installed; if a capability is
missing, say so and do it by hand. Cut vague corporate phrasing on sight.
One-to-one outreach belongs to the `email` skill; broadcast belongs here.
